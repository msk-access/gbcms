Methodology

Fragment Origin Signal Classification

Each variant is classified based on three signals:

  • TUMOR-LIKE: Sub-nucleosomal enrichment >1.3, KS p<0.05, and not in the CH gene set.
  • CH-LIKE: Known CH gene, enrichment <1.2, and KS p>0.05 (ALT distribution mirrors REF).
  • AMBIGUOUS: Mixed criteria — does not clearly fit either category.
  • INSUFFICIENT: ALT fragment count below threshold (min_alt=3).

Sub-nucleosomal Enrichment

Ratio of ALT fragments <150bp to REF fragments <150bp. ctDNA tends to show enrichment (ratio >1.0) due to tumor-derived fragments being shorter. CH mirrors background cfDNA.

Glossary

  • REF n / ALT n: Fragment count classified as reference or alternate allele.
  • Δ(ALT−REF): Difference in mean fragment size (bp). Negative = ALT shorter.
  • KS p: Kolmogorov-Smirnov test p-value comparing ALT vs REF size distributions.
  • LLR: Log-likelihood ratio (positive = tumor-like fragment profile).
  • Sub-nuc enrich.: ALT(<150bp fraction) / REF(<150bp fraction).
  • Density overlay: Gaussian KDE (Silverman bandwidth) normalized to unit area, plotted on the right y-axis. Enables direct shape comparison between REF and ALT regardless of fragment count differences.
